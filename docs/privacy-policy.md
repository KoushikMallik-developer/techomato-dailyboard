# DailyBoard privacy policy

DailyBoard is made by techomato. This page says what the app stores and how to delete it.

## What the app stores
- **On your phone:** everything you enter (habits, tasks, notes, events, bills, subscriptions, documents, medicines, trips, lists, projects, health and water logs) and your settings.
- **In your account (Google Firebase):** your email address, and one backup copy of the same data so you can use it on another phone. The backup is readable only by your own signed-in account.
- **Sign in with Google:** if you choose it, Google tells us your email address. We do not get your password.

## What the app does not do
- No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
- We do not sell or share your data.
- Your data is not read by us. It is stored for you, and Google Firebase processes it for us as our hosting provider.

## Shared phones
When someone signs in with a different account on the same phone, a copy of the earlier data stays on the phone, out of sight, until the app is erased or uninstalled. Deleting your account removes that copy.

## Notifications
Reminders and alarms are scheduled on your phone from your own data. They do not use a server.

## Deleting your data
- **Erase all data** (Settings → Account and data) clears the app on your phone, and the backup follows on the next sync.
- **Delete account** (Settings → Account and data) deletes your backup and your account from Firebase for good.
- If you cannot open the app, write to the address below and we will delete it for you.
